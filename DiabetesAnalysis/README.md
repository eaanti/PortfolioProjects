# Diabetes Risk Factor Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes diabetes prevalence and associated demographic, health, lifestyle, and healthcare factors using health indicator data.

## Tools Used

* **SQL Server** – Data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory analysis
* **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards and visualization

## Analysis

The project explored diabetes prevalence across factors including:

* Age and gender
* Income and education
* BMI and general health
* Blood pressure, heart disease, and stroke
* Physical activity and lifestyle factors

## Key Insights

* Diabetes prevalence was approximately **13%** among participants.
* Higher prevalence was observed among older adults and individuals with obesity, high blood pressure, and heart disease.
* Poorer general health and lower physical activity levels were associated with higher diabetes prevalence.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL and Tableau to analyze healthcare data and generate insights into factors associated with diabetes prevalence.
